How it works

  1. 1

    Detects the script

    From the characters you enter, including historical scripts like Modi, Sharada and Grantha.

  2. 2

    Routes it to the right method

    A neural model for 21 modern languages, our verified character mapping for Modi text, and an open vision model for handwritten Modi pages.

  3. 3

    Ranks the candidate readings

    Several readings per word, re-ranked to prefer real, common words where word lists exist.

  4. 4

    Shows its doubts

    Words the model is unsure of are marked, using a threshold chosen on validation data and tested on unseen words. Your corrections are saved.

Tested on 20 languages (300 unseen words each) and 60 unseen manuscript pages. Code and results on GitHub.
