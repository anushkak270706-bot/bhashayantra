How it works
  1. Detects the script of what you enter, including historical scripts like Modi.
  2. Routes it to the right method: a neural model for 21 modern languages, our verified character mapping for Modi.
  3. Ranks the top candidate readings for every word, preferring real, common words.
  4. Shows its doubts. Words it isn't sure about are marked; you can pick another reading, and your correction is saved.

Tested on 20 languages with 300 held-out words each. Code and results: GitHub.
